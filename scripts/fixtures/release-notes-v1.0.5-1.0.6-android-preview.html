What's new

Versions 1.0.5 & 1.0.6

Two updates since our last note: safer trading tools and account controls in 1.0.5, then a clearer Home, readability pass, and dark mode in 1.0.6.

Version 1.0.5

Get notified for Offers and catalog decisions, report or block collectors, and delete your account in Settings.

Push for Offers and catalog — Optional push notifications when someone sends an Offer (trade or for sale) or when a catalog submission is decided. Control Push: Offers and Push: Catalog in Settings.

Report or block — On another collector’s profile, use ⋯ to report spam, scams, or harassment, or block them. Blocked collectors cannot start new Offers with you.

Delete your account — Settings → Delete Account removes your sign-in and vault photos in the app. Approved catalog entries may stay in the community database without your name.

Sale offers in Activity — New and answered sale offers now show in Activity (and can push) the same way trades do — not only in Hunt → Offers.

Version 1.0.6

Home is a clearer launch pad, the whole app is easier to read with less clutter, and dark mode is ready when you want it.

Home launch pad — Home is rebuilt as a dashboard — Keep Going plus Go to tiles for Vault, Discover, Hunt, Offers, Boards, Badges, Profile, Activity, and Settings.

Full UI revamp — Larger type, stronger color contrast, and less chrome clutter across Home, Hunt, Offers, and pin screens — so the app feels calmer and easier to scan.

Dark mode — Choose light, dark, or system in Settings. Screens, badges, and accents stay readable after dark.

Smarter pin matching — Tighter matching when you link pins to the catalog or find existing vault copies — less guesswork when you add or connect a pin.
